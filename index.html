About us

Making everyday shopping easier.

EKUTHULENI SERVICE is a customer-focused shopping and delivery service created to help people obtain the things they need without having to travel to town themselves.

Our service is built around a simple idea: you should not always have to leave home to get what you need.

The problem we solve

✓Long or inconvenient trips to town
✓Transport costs and time spent travelling
✓Busy work, study or family responsibilities
✓Difficulty carrying heavy purchases
✓Customers who need a more accessible option
Our services

What can we help you get?

We can assist with a broad range of approved everyday purchases. Availability may depend on the item, supplier and delivery requirements.

Groceries

Household Items

Personal Care

Larger Purchases

How it works

A simple five-step process.

No complicated app is required. We start with direct, human customer service.

01

Send your request

Provide your shopping list, quantities, preferences and instructions.

02

We confirm

We review the request and confirm costs, availability and applicable fees.

03

We purchase

Our representative purchases the approved items.

04

We record

Receipts and purchase details are retained as transaction records.

05

We deliver

Your items are brought to the agreed delivery location.
